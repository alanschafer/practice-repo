Soft, floral, and calming. This is the cup we reach for once the fire burns low. Whole chamomile flowers bring a gentle honeyed sweetness, lifted with bright lemon peel and just a touch of lavender.

Steep one heaping teaspoon per cup in just-boiled water for 5 minutes.
