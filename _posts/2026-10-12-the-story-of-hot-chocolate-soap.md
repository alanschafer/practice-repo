It started, like most good ideas, beside the fire. A mug of cocoa, a pot of soap curing on the shelf, and the thought: what if a bar could smell like this?

## Getting the swirl right

The first batches tasted the idea but missed the look. It took several tries to get the whipped vanilla top to sit just right on the cocoa base, soft peaks and all.

> The best things from the hearth are slow. Soap is no different.

## How it's made

1. Melt cocoa butter, shea, and coconut oil.
2. Swirl the cocoa base by hand and pipe the whipped top.
3. Dust with real cocoa and cure for six weeks.

Every bar is cut by hand, so each one is a little different.
