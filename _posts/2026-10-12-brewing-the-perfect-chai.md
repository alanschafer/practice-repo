Our Hearthside Chai is bold enough to steep like any black tea, but it really shines when you simmer it on the stove.

## Stovetop chai for two

1. Bring **1 cup of water** to a simmer with **2 heaping teaspoons** of chai.
2. Simmer for 3 minutes, until the water turns deep amber.
3. Add **1 cup of milk** and a spoonful of honey.
4. Warm gently for 2 more minutes. Don't let it boil.
5. Strain into two mugs.

> The spices open up in the heat. That's where the magic is.

## Make it yours

- **Spicier:** add a slice of fresh ginger while it simmers.
- **Creamier:** use whole milk or oat milk.
- **Sweeter:** stir in brown sugar instead of honey.
