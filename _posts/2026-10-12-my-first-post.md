Hello. This is my first Jekyll post, and the start of a small journal about learning to build things on the web.

## How posts work

To write a new post, add a file to the `_posts` folder named like `YYYY-MM-DD-title.md`, with front matter at the top:

```yaml
---
title: "A New Post"
lede: "A one-line summary that appears under the title."
tags: [notes]
---
```

Push it to GitHub, wait about a minute, and it appears here.
