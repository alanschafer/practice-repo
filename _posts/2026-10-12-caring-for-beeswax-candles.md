Beeswax is one of the oldest candle materials there is, and with a little care it burns beautifully for hours.

## Simple habits

- **Trim the wick** to about a quarter inch before each burn.
- **Keep it out of drafts** so the flame stays steady and drip-free.
- **Let it cool** before moving it, so the wax sets evenly.

## A note on bloom

Over time, beeswax can develop a soft white film called bloom. It's completely natural. Just buff it away with a soft cloth to bring back the shine.
