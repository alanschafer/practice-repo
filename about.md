## Where it began

For centuries, the hearth was the center of the home. It was where bread was baked, stories were told, and cold hands were warmed around a hot cup. Shay's Hearth is built around that feeling.

## What we make

- **Soap**, cold-processed and cured for weeks, like our Hot Chocolate and Oat Milk & Honey bars.
- **Hot cocoa**, rich Dutch-process mixes layered in corked jars, classic or peppermint.
- **Tea**, loose-leaf blends with whole spices and real flowers, from bold chai to calming chamomile.

## How we make it

Slowly. Every product is made in small batches, finished by hand, and packed in jars, linen, and kraft paper.

> This is a sample shop. Products, prices, and stories are placeholders while the shop takes shape.
